A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Before you plan a real journey, confirm availability and details directly with a verified provider.

Are the fares and schedules live?

No. This website is a front-end prototype. Routes, prices, times and available seats are sample data for illustration, not operator inventory.

Can I pay for a trip here?

No. Payment processing is not connected. A prototype booking is saved in this browser and marked pending payment; no successful payment or valid reservation is implied.

Where do students meet at AUSU?

The campus pickup point is not confirmed and appears as a placeholder. Please verify actual pickup and departure information with the transport provider.

Is Aleesha run by Al-Istiqama University?

No. Aleesha Travel & Tours is presented as an independent transport-service concept. The site does not claim university operation, partnership or endorsement.
